# GOOD: UI (adopt this)

Verdict from the owner: **GOOD**. Every menu in every game should look and move like this.
See also [[UI Spec Sheet]], [[UI Motion]], [[BAD - Avoid]].

## Simulator shop / menu look
Sources: Mythic Egg shop ("made in Figma"), brainrot Lucky Block / Cash Shark shop, @jubydandev "custom ui w/ claude" (Digsite / Pen / Sell game).
- Thick saturated title bar (red, yellow, blue or lime). An oversized 3D icon breaks out of the top-left corner, tilted about -8° to -12°. Big white heavy-outlined title.
- Chunky square red close X with a white X and a dark outline.
- Dark navy / purple / charcoal panel body so the cards pop.
- Every card is a fully saturated colour block: gradient, sunburst rays, tiled LEGO-stud texture. A big glossy 3D item render fills 60–70% of it.
- Lime-green pill price buttons: Robux icon plus a white outlined number, a darker 3D lip underneath and a gloss stripe on top. The rainbow-gradient button is for the best-value tier.
- A purple gift button beside every price.
- Rarity colour-coding: animated rainbow / neon border for Mythic, "???" dark silhouettes for undiscovered items, % odds tiles.
- Angled corner tags: New!, EXCLUSIVE, SECRET, MYTHIC!, LIMITED!, "Very PRO deal!!", BEST DEAL!, SAVE 19%.
- States: dark gray LOCKED!, green CLAIMED, green EQUIP.
- Progress everywhere: bars, "Found 5/52 pets", "CLAIM +3% LUCK!", streak 1/7.
- HUD: coloured pill nav buttons across the top (Digsite blue / Pen green / Sell pink, UPGRADES), a numbered hotbar at the bottom, Store / Index buttons on the left, Eggs / Pets on the right, power and cash counters bottom-left with "+" buttons that open the shop.
- Standard menus: Shop (Featured, Gamepasses, Potions, Cash), Upgrades tiers (x2 → x1,250), 7-day Daily Rewards with an oversized Day 7, Playtime Rewards with timers, Pet Index with collection rewards.

## Themed shop frames
- **Match the game's theme.** @.6ix13's sky / jet game uses cloud puffs on the corners, a title bar patterned with jet silhouettes, a magenta X and a cloud-poof animation on open / scroll.
- **Reskin for events.** @ekxu2's Halloween version adds a witch hat on the shop icon, a spiderweb over the X, pumpkins and ghosts hanging off the edges, an orange→purple sunset title bar and falling orange particles.
- Sections to copy: Exclusives (LIMITED TIME ONLY!, old price struck through), Passes (STARTER PACK! BEST DEAL!), a gamepass grid (x2 Cash, VIP crown, x2 Egg Growth), timed boosts (1x > 2x, last 15 minutes), x100 Cash / Speed tiers with chest art that grows each tier, a bundle with an exclusive Title + Aura + Pet "not in eggs or rolls", a live "Ends in 7d 00h 01m 15s" timer, and permanent upgrades with huge numbers (+2.56Qn Power).

## Brainrot shop layout (Ride a Brainrot)
Featured lucky block with an odds strip and pity ("Phoenix guaranteed in 75 blocks"), 1 / 3 / 10 bundles with SAVE %, an exclusive shop-only brainrot, server luck boosts (15 MIN, everyone in the server benefits), stamina, and 5 tiers of cash packs whose art gets bigger each tier.
