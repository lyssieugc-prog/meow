## Worlds
- **Map generation (@cordhub_, "Claude + Codex, 15 min"):** an island with a central round sand spawn plaza, about six dirt paths radiating out, a river ring with wooden bridges, terraced pine cliffs with a waterfall, then ocean. Open centre for gameplay, dense decoration at the edges. Build as a seeded generator script (noise terrain + flattened centre, spline paths, carved river, scatter with spacing rules).
- **Steal a Dragon (Oryvex):** bright daytime, stylised cliffs, low-poly cone trees (green and pink), stone-castle player bases with grids of dragon pods, red-carpet roads with yellow chevrons and gold torches, a glowing portal shop, dragons with floating name + rarity + price tags and wing trails.
