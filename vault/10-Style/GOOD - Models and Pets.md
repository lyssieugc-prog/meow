# GOOD: Models, Pets, Bosses

See also [[BAD - Avoid]] and [[Cutscenes]].

## The rule
Keep the chunky, blocky / low-poly Roblox look, but get detail from **many small repeated pieces** (feathers, scales, spikes, teeth, tentacles, voxels). Give each model **one strong theme or element**. Use a **dark, muted body with bright glowing accents** (eyes, core, cracks, edges). Add built-in VFX: glow, particles, orbiting props. Idle animation always.
